Hi, thanks for stopping by.

## Why this blog

At work I learn a lot, some from docs, some only after getting it wrong.
But what I don't write down, I forget. And what I don't share stays with me alone.

So this blog is my notebook, and a place to share with anyone on a similar path.

## What I'll write about

- **Knowledge** I look into and sum up, as plainly as I can.
- **Experience** from work: how I solve problems, my mistakes, and what I learned.
- **Notes** on tools and ways of working.

This is how I see things at the time of writing, and it surely has gaps.

## Your feedback matters

If something is wrong, unclear, or could be done better, please [let me know](/en/contact/).
Every honest comment helps me grow.

See you in the next post.
